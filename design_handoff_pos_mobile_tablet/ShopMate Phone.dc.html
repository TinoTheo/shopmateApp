What changes at EXPANDED
The rail expands back to the full 84dp sidebar with labels, and the header gains the centred clock. POS becomes the three fixed zones — product area, 1px divider, 240dp sale ticket with the number pad always visible. Tap a ticket line to select it, then type a quantity; loose goods need manual entry on nearly every sale.
Everywhere else the phone's bottom sheet becomes a right-hand panel pinned to the sheet edge — same content, two containers. On Stock that panel is where "why does it say 6?" gets answered, as a ledger.
Medium behaves as Expanded. There is no third bucket, and POS never reflows between them.
