3Copy and states pass: sync, plurals, sign-in, run-out, reports
Layouts are unchanged. Only the copy and state treatments changed. The copy sheet (3a) is the source of truth, and the same strings with plurals are in copy/strings_copy_pass.xml. The sync, sign-in and tablet screenshots didn’t come through with the upload, so those screens follow the brief’s text and the visual language of the stock, POS and reports shots.
Copy sheetEvery string, with one/other forms and zero cases, keyed by where it appears

1 · Sync history
Sale row title
sync_row_sale_title
Sale · {amount}
Meta: {receipt} · {HH:mm}
Name it by what the person controls: which sale and how much, not “Sale sync”.

sync_row_snapshot_title
Product list

Name it by what the person controls: what was sent, not “Store snapshot”.

Row status
sync_status_*
Waiting · Sending… · Sent · Paused · Not sent
Not sent is the only one in ErrorRed. Waiting and Paused are grey (Offline is a state, not an error).
409, renumbering
sync_reason_renumbering
Number already used on another till. Renumbering…
Failure says what happened and what to do. Not styled as an error: the app is fixing it.
409, resolved
sync_reason_renumbered
Meta: Sent as {newReceipt} · {HH:mm}
Line: Its first number, {oldReceipt}, was already used on another till. The customer’s paper receipt still shows the first number.
Says what happened and what is now true. Shows as Sent with a green dot.
403, not allowed
sync_reason_forbidden
This account isn’t allowed to {change stock | change prices | record sales}, so your store didn’t accept it. {The 24 loaves are | It’s} still counted on this till.
What to do: Ask the owner to turn on {stock changes | price changes} for your account, then tap Send again.
Failure says what happened and what to do. The cashier’s next step is to ask the owner.
401, signed out
sync_reason_signed_out
Row status: Paused
Meta: Sends once you sign in
Agrees with the “Sending is paused” card. Not a failure.
5xx / timeout
sync_reason_no_answer
Your store didn’t answer. Trying again by itself.
Offline is a state, not an error. Stays Waiting; never red.
Other 4xx
sync_reason_other
Your store didn’t accept this {sale | change}. It’s still saved on this till.
What to do: Tap Send again. If it’s refused again, open Details and send them to whoever set up ShopMate.
Failure says what happened and what to do, even when the cause is unknown.

sync_details_*
Toggle: Details
Box label: For the owner or support
Raw server text, then {kind} · {date}, {HH:mm} · Till {n}
Button: Copy details → toast Details copied
Collapsed by default, set apart in a bordered box under the plain text. Inter only, 12sp, ShopBlackMid.

3 · Sign-in status bar
Sign in and onboarding
—
Decision: a Trade Green top band (1j layout) with the stacked logo directly on green, then a white sheet with a 24dp top-start corner. White status-bar icons stay correct everywhere.
Open the till · {store} · Till {n} · Enter your 4-digit till code · Opening the till · Wrong code: That code didn’t match. Try again.
Onboarding: Create your shop · You can change these later in Settings. · Shop name · Your name · Country · Create shop
One status-bar rule for the whole app, so there is no per-screen icon switching. The stacked-logo tile is dropped because the layout changed: the logo now sits on the band itself.
4 · Run-out language (one function, list and detail call it)
Out
runout_out
List: Out of stock (plus the black Out chip)
Detail: Out since {Tuesday | 14 Oct}
Black chip, as shipped.
Runs out before closing
runout_today
Runs out today
StockOrange. Compared by calendar date, not by rounding a day count.
Next calendar day
runout_tomorrow
Runs out tomorrow
Replaces both “1 days left” and “0 days left”.
2–6 days
runout_day
Runs out {Saturday … Wednesday}
CLAUDE.md prefers the day name over a count.
7–13 days
runout_week
Lasts over a week
Never show a number as certain when it isn’t: “over” gives a floor, not a date.
14–27 days
runout_weeks
otherLasts over %d weeks
Floor of days / 7. No one form: the smallest value is 2.
28+ days
runout_month
Lasts over a month
Never show a number as certain when it isn’t.
Too little history
runout_no_history
List: Not enough sales to tell yet
Detail basis: {n} sold since it was added on {d MMM}
Says why there is no date.
Dormant
runout_dormant
oneNot sold since yesterday
otherNot sold in %d days
Plurals: one/other in plurals.xml.
Detail basis line
runout_basis
Selling about {n} a day this week
Shows where the date comes from. “About” because it is a rate.
Rule
—
runOutDate = now + stock ÷ velocity. The phrase comes from (runOutDate.toLocalDate() − today) in days. The list, the detail and the strip all read the same ProductInsight.runOut value. Never format it twice.
Fixes the list saying 1 and the detail saying 0.
